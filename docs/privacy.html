Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Shiduo (拾掇) is a macOS app made by Zhipeng Han, an independent developer ("we"). Short version: your files never leave your Mac. Shiduo has no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

What Shiduo does on your Mac

Shiduo reads the files and folders you give it access to (for example Desktop, Documents and Downloads) so it can show, search and organize them. All of this happens locally on your Mac: the search index, thumbnails, text recognition in images (Apple Vision), photo content recognition, and duplicate detection (SHA-256 fingerprints and Vision feature prints). Files that exist only in iCloud are skipped and are not downloaded.

When Shiduo connects to the internet

  • Update check: Shiduo may ask GitHub for the latest release of this project. GitHub receives a normal web request, including your IP address.
  • License check (Shiduo Pro only): when you activate, verify or deactivate a license, Shiduo sends the license key, a random installation ID and your Mac's name to our license server (hosted on Cloudflare), which checks it with Creem. This happens at most once every 24 hours. No file names or file contents are ever sent.

Purchases

Shiduo Pro is sold through Creem (creem.io), which acts as merchant of record. Creem collects your name, email, billing details and payment information to process the order, under Creem's privacy policy. We receive your email, name and order details so we can provide support and manage your license. We never see your full card details.

What we store

For each license we store the key, its activation status and installation ID. If you email us, we keep your message to answer it. We do not sell or share personal data with anyone else.

Your choices

You can use Shiduo Free without ever activating a license. You can deactivate your license at any time in the app, and you can ask us to delete the data we hold about you by emailing 694561981@qq.com.

Children

Shiduo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
