Shiduo Pro license help

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Shiduo Pro is a $9.90 one-time purchase. Here is everything about activating, moving and checking your license.

Activate Shiduo Pro

  1. After paying, copy the license key from the confirmation page or the receipt email from Creem.
  2. Open Shiduo, choose Shiduo › Settings… (⌘,) and open the Pro tab.
  3. Paste the key and click Activate. Pro features unlock right away.

Not sure yet? Click Start 7-Day Free Trial in the same place. The trial starts only when you click it. No sign-up and no payment details are needed.

Can’t find your license key?

  • Search your inbox (and spam folder) for the receipt email from Creem; the key is in it.
  • Or sign in to the Creem customer portal with the email you paid with. Your orders and license key are there.
  • Still stuck? Email 694561981@qq.com with the email address you used to order, and we’ll send the key again.

Moving to a new Mac

A license works on one Mac at a time. Before you switch, open Settings › Pro on the old Mac and click Deactivate This Mac, then activate the same key on the new Mac. If the old Mac is lost or already erased, email 694561981@qq.com with your order email and we’ll free up the activation.

Offline use and license checks

  • Shiduo checks the license at most once every 24 hours, and only when you have Pro.
  • If your Mac is offline, Pro keeps working for up to 30 days since the last successful check. Connect once to keep going.
  • The check sends only the license key, a random installation ID and your Mac’s name to our license server, which verifies it with Creem. No file names or file contents are ever sent. See the Privacy Policy.

Refunds

Within 14 days of purchase you can get a full refund, no questions asked. See the Refund Policy for how to request one.

Contact

Anything else: 694561981@qq.com

如何激活拾掇 Pro

  1. 付款后，在付款完成页或 Creem 发来的收据邮件里拷贝授权码。
  2. 打开拾掇，选择拾掇 › 设置…（⌘,），切到「Pro」标签页。
  3. 粘贴授权码，点「激活」，Pro 功能马上解锁。

还想再考虑？在同一个地方点「开始 7 天免费试用」，点了才开始计时，不用注册，也不填付款信息。

找不到授权码？

  • 在邮箱（包括垃圾邮件）里搜 Creem 发来的收据邮件，授权码就在里面。
  • 或者用付款时的邮箱登录 Creem 客户中心，订单和授权码都能看到。
  • 还是找不到？发邮件到 694561981@qq.com，附上下单邮箱，我们帮你重新发送。

换 Mac / 解绑

一个授权同一时间可在一台 Mac 上使用。换 Mac 前，在旧 Mac 上打开设置 › Pro，点「解绑此 Mac」，再到新 Mac 上用同一个授权码激活。旧 Mac 丢了或已经抹掉？发邮件到 694561981@qq.com，附上下单邮箱，我们帮你释放激活名额。

离线使用与授权校验

  • 只有使用 Pro 时才会校验授权，最多每 24 小时一次。
  • Mac 没联网时，距离上次校验成功 30 天内 Pro 都能照常使用；联网一次就能继续。
  • 校验只把授权码、一个随机生成的安装 ID 和你的 Mac 名称发到我们的授权服务器，由它向 Creem 核实。绝不会发送任何文件名或文件内容。详见隐私政策。
